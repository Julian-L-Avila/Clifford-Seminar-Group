\(\Cl(V,q) = T(V)/\mathcal{I}_q\)

Clifford Algebras in Theoretical Physics Álgebras de Clifford en la Física Teórica

Coordinate-free linear spaces, Lie & Spin symmetries, Kepler dynamics, and relativistic spacetime algebra

Espacios lineales libres de coordenadas, simetrías de Lie y Spin, dinámica de Kepler y álgebra del espaciotiempo

Abstract. This working seminar investigates invariant geometric formulations of theoretical physics through real Clifford algebras \(\Cl(V,q)\). Anchored by the Kepler–Coulomb problem as a comparative dynamical backbone, our progression moves from coordinate-free dual and quadratic spaces, coupled oscillators, and bivector Lie algebras \(\Cl^2(V,q) \cong \mathfrak{so}(p,q)\) to \(\Spin(p,q)\) rotors, Kustaanheimo–Stiefel regularization, spacetime algebra \(\Cl(1,3)\), Gauge Theory Gravity, and the Dirac–Hestenes equation. Sessions emphasize live blackboard derivations, critical peer commentary, and archival research documentation.

Resumen. Este seminario de trabajo investiga formulaciones geométricas invariantes de la física teórica mediante álgebras de Clifford reales \(\Cl(V,q)\). Articulado en torno al problema de Kepler–Coulomb como eje dinámico comparativo, el recorrido abarca desde espacios duales y cuadráticos libres de coordenadas, osciladores acoplados y álgebras de Lie de bivectores \(\Cl^2(V,q) \cong \mathfrak{so}(p,q)\), hasta rotores de \(\Spin(p,q)\), regularización de Kustaanheimo–Stiefel, álgebra del espaciotiempo \(\Cl(1,3)\), gravedad como teoría gauge y la ecuación de Dirac–Hestenes.

Session 01 · Plenary Theses & Protocol Summary

Dual Spaces, Multilinear Forms, and Quadratic Spaces

Thesenpapier 01 (PDF) Protocol 2026-11-04 (PDF)
Thesis I. Identifying velocities with canonical momenta via Cartesian indices (\(p_i = m\dot{x}_i\)) obscures the duality between \(V\) and \(V^*\), hiding the geometric foundation of Hamiltonian mechanics and relativistic field theories.
Thesis II. The free tensor algebra \(T(V)\) carries excess relation-free degrees of freedom; physical geometry only emerges after factoring out the ideal generated by measurement—the quadratic form \(q(v)\).
Thesis III. A metric is not an intrinsic primitive of a linear space, but an auxiliary constitutive mapping \(b^\flat : V \to V^*\) converting kinematic displacements into linear measurement functionals.
Thesis IV. Causal structure in relativistic physics is an algebraic consequence of signature \((1,3)\) on a four-dimensional real linear space, prior to differential geometry or pseudo-Riemannian metric tensors.
Resolved on 4 November 2026 (Scribe: Sebastián Rodríguez): Dual bases transform by \(\tilde{\omega}^i = (T^{-1})^*\omega^i\) directly from invariance of the pairing \(\pair{\tilde{\omega}^j}{\tilde{e}_i} = \delta^j_i\); no canonical \(V \cong V^*\) exists outside isometries of \(b\), whereas \(\ev: V \to V^{**}\) is basis-independent and natural (\(T^{**}\circ\ev = \ev\circ T\)). For Next Session: Prepare Problem 4 (\(p = m\,b^\flat(v)\), \(b^\sharp\), and \(H = \tfrac{1}{2m}b^*(p,p)\)).

Multivector structures, Lie algebras, and Spin groups
 Module isomorphism \(\Cl(V,q) \cong \bigwedge V\), blade projections, bivector Lie algebra \(\Cl^2(V,q) \cong \mathfrak{so}(p,q)\), Clifford and Lipschitz groups, \(\Pin(p,q)\), \(\Spin(p,q)\), and rotor transformations \(v \mapsto Rv\tilde{R}\). VR, DL

Clifford analysis and monogenic fields
 Vector derivative \(\nabla\), generalized Cauchy–Riemann operator, monogenic multivector fields \(\nabla\Phi = 0\), Cauchy kernel, Borel–Pompeiu integral formula, and coordinate-free multipole expansions of extended Keplerian sources. HS, DL
Part B: Classical Kepler Dynamics in \(\Cl(3,0)\)
05
Central forces and orbital angular momentum
 Equation of motion \(\dot{p} = -(k/r^3)r\), orbital angular momentum bivector \(L = r \wedge p\), conservation proof \(\dot{L} = 0\), planar confinement \(r \wedge L = 0\), coordinate-free duality via volume pseudoscalar \(I_3\). Hestenes (NFCM)
06
Laplace–Runge–Lenz vector and conics
 Multivector construction \(A = \frac{1}{mk}pL - \hat{r}\), algebraic proof of \(\dot{A} = 0\) without coordinate frames, orbit derivation via scalar contraction \(r \cdot A\), and invariant classification of conics. Hestenes (NFCM)
Part C: Regularization, Harmonic Analysis, and Hidden Symmetries
07
Kustaanheimo–Stiefel transformation
 Regularization of collision singularities at \(r \to 0\), spinor mapping \(x = u\sigma_3\tilde{u}\) with \(u \in \Cl^+(3,0) \cong \mathbb{H}\), Sundman time rescaling \(\dd t = r\,\dd s\), and \(U(1)\) gauge freedom. Hestenes, DL
08
Four-dimensional oscillator and \(\SO(4)\)
 Linearization to \(u'' + \frac{|E|}{2}u = 0\), four-dimensional isotropic harmonic oscillator, realization of \(\mathfrak{so}(4) \cong \mathfrak{su}(2) \oplus \mathfrak{su}(2)\) dynamical symmetry via \(L\) and \(A\), bound-state degeneracy. DL, Hestenes
09
Clifford–Fourier analysis and Fock projection
 Clifford–Fourier transform with pseudoscalar kernel \(e^{-I_3 k \cdot x}\), multivector uncertainty relations, Fock stereographic projection of the Coulomb problem onto \(S^3\), and bound states as spherical monogenics. HS
Part D: Relativistic Dynamics in Spacetime Algebra \(\Cl(1,3)\)
10
Spacetime algebra and observer split
 Dirac generators \(\{\gamma_\mu\}\), signature \((+,-,-,-)\), observer split via \(\gamma_0\), relative spatial bivectors \(\sigma_k = \gamma_k\gamma_0\), proper Lorentz rotors in \(\Spin^+(1,3)\), rotor kinematics \(\dot{R} = \frac{1}{2}\Omega R\), Thomas precession. Hestenes (STA), DL
11
Relativistic electrodynamics and Lorentz force
 Spacetime vector derivative \(\nabla = \gamma^\mu\partial_\mu\), Faraday bivector \(F = E + I_4 B\), Maxwell system \(\nabla F = J\), relativistic equation of motion \(\dot{p} = e F \cdot u\), proper-time rotor integration. Hestenes (STA), DL, Girard
12
Relativistic central forces and perihelion advance
 Relativistic central potentials, breakdown of LRL vector conservation, energy-dependent dynamical corrections, and calculation of special-relativistic perihelion advance. DL, Hestenes
13
Gauge Theory Gravity and planetary orbits
 Position gauge field \(\bar{h}(a)\) and rotation gauge field \(\bar{\Omega}(a)\) on Minkowski background, spherically symmetric solutions, geodesic rotor trajectories, and general-relativistic orbital precession. DL
14
Geometric thermodynamics in spacetime algebra
 Conserved particle flux \(N = nu\), stress-energy operator \(T(a)\), entropy current \(S = su\), invariant thermal 1-vector \(\beta = u/(k_{\mathrm{B}}T)\), Killing equilibrium \(\nabla \wedge \beta = 0\), and Tolman–Ehrenfest gradients. DL, STA literature
Part E: Quantum Kepler Problem and Dirac–Hestenes Theory
15
Real Pauli algebra and spatial spin
 Spatial algebra \(\Cl(3,0)\) without matrix representations, unit spatial vectors as spin observables, Pauli multivector wave equation, spin-orbit coupling from the geometric product. DL, Hestenes
16
Dirac–Hestenes field equation
 Spacetime algebra representation of the Dirac equation, even multivector field \(\Psi \in \Cl^+(1,3)\), spin plane \(\gamma_2\gamma_1\), polar form \(\Psi = \rho^{1/2}e^{I_4\beta/2}R\), current \(J = \Psi\gamma_0\tilde{\Psi}\), spin bivector \(S = \frac{1}{2}\hbar\Psi\gamma_3\tilde{\Psi}\). DL, Hestenes (STA), VR
17
Exact solution of the relativistic hydrogen atom
 Separation of variables in spherical coordinates within \(\Cl^+(1,3)\), rotor spherical harmonics, radial differential equations in the even subalgebra, derivation of the Sommerfeld–Dirac energy spectrum. DL
18
Fine structure, Darwin term, and zitterbewegung
 Non-relativistic expansion of the Dirac–Hestenes equation, geometric origin of the Darwin correction, rotor phase dynamics, lightlike field circulation, field-theoretic interpretation of zitterbewegung. DL, Hestenes

Section 03

Seminar Methodology & Colloquium Format Metodología y Estructura del Coloquio

Inspired by the German academic research seminar tradition, sessions reject passive slide lectures in favor of direct blackboard derivations, structured critical response, and permanent written documentation.

Inspirado en la tradición alemana del seminario académico de investigación, cada sesión privilegia la derivación directa en tablero, la réplica crítica estructurada y la documentación archivística permanente.

Referat

Lead Blackboard Exposition

Delivered jointly by the assigned senior–junior tandem. Focuses on step-by-step blackboard derivations of primary equations, foregrounding conceptual hypotheses and algebraic transitions over projected slides.

Exposición conjunta del tándem asignado centrada en derivaciones paso a paso en tablero, priorizando las hipótesis conceptuales y transiciones algebraicas sobre diapositivas proyectadas.

Korreferat

Respondent Critique

A designated respondent delivers critical commentary right after the Referat, testing implicit assumptions, contrasting results with matrix/coordinate formalisms, identifying pathologies, and opening debate.

Un participante asignado presenta una réplica crítica tras el Referat: examina hipótesis implícitas, contrasta con formalismos matriciales o coordenados y formula preguntas guía.

Thesenpapier

Position Paper (≥ 5 Days Prior)

A concise memorandum circulated by the presenting tandem at least five days before meeting, containing formal definitions, key algebraic identities, preparatory exercises, and explicit theses for plenary debate.

Memorando conciso circulado al menos cinco días antes de la sesión con definiciones formales, identidades clave, ejercicios preparatorios y tesis explícitas para el debate plenario.

Sitzungsprotokoll

Session Record (≤ 7 Days After)

A rotating scribe documents blackboard derivations, corrections, resolved debates, and open problems, archiving the finalized protocol in this repository within seven days of the session.

Un relator rotativo registra las derivaciones en tablero, correcciones, debates resueltos y preguntas abiertas, archivando el acta definitiva en el repositorio en los siete días posteriores.

Phase Duration Content Responsible
I 40 min Live blackboard derivation (Referat) Presenting tandem
II 10 min Critical response & opening theses (Korreferat) Respondent
III 35 min Plenary debate & physical interpretation All participants
IV 5 min Synthesis of open questions & preview Seminar leader

Tandem Mentorship & Preparation Cadence Mentoría en Tándem y Cadencia Preparatoria

Starting with Topic 2, expositions pair a senior participant with a junior collaborator to share blackboard duties equally while fostering peer mentorship in multilinear algebra and relativistic dynamics.

Desde el Tema 2, cada exposición empareja un participante senior con un colaborador junior para compartir el tablero en igualdad de condiciones y fomentar la mentoría entre pares.

Preparatory meetings with the seminar leader take place two weeks and five days before each session. Every attendee submits at least one written question on the Thesenpapier 24 hours prior to meeting.

Las reuniones preparatorias con el guía ocurren dos semanas y cinco días antes de cada sesión. Cada asistente envía al menos una pregunta escrita sobre el Thesenpapier 24 horas antes.
